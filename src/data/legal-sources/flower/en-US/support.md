# Support

Status: Confirmed

Flower Island: Living Sky lets you watch flowers and a clock on a floating island, with the sky changing with the time and weather.

## Contact

Open Settings in the app, tap ⓘ (About This App) at the top right, then "Send Feedback." To ask for a new flower, tap "Request" at the top right of the Collection.

Because your name and contact details aren't sent, we can't reply individually. We read everything we receive and use it to improve the app.

## FAQ

**My purchased flower doesn't appear.**

Go to Settings → ⓘ (About This App) → "Restore Purchases." You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

**The weather doesn't match where I am.**

Turn on "Match My Local Weather" in Settings and allow location access. Local weather applies only while "Follow Current Time" is on.

**There's no sound.**

By default, the app is silent while your device is in Silent mode. To hear it in Silent mode, turn off "Mute in Silent Mode" in the Sound tab of Settings.
