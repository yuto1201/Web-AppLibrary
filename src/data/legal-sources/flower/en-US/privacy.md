# Privacy Policy

Status: Confirmed

This policy explains what information Flower Island ("the app") handles and how. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, or similar details.

## Location

The app uses your device's approximate location only when "Use My Location for Sun and Moon" or "Match My Local Weather" is turned on in Settings.

Sun and moon positions are calculated on your device.

Weather is fetched by sending your location to Apple WeatherKit, where it is handled under Apple's privacy policy.

The developer never receives your location, and the app does not store it.

## Purchases

Flowers are purchased through the App Store. Payment information is never sent to the developer. Purchase records are kept by the App Store.

## Feedback and Flower Requests

Only when you send feedback or a flower request, the following reaches the developer:

What you wrote (the type and message; for a flower request, the flower's name and your note)

The app version and build number, OS version, device model, and language and region settings

This is delivered through a program made for the app that runs on Cloudflare, stored in a private place only the developer can see (GitHub), and used to improve the app. Your name, contact details, and device identifiers are not sent, so we can't reply to you individually.

Your IP address is used only to prevent too many submissions in a short time, and is not stored.

If a submission can't be sent, for example because you're offline, it is saved on your device and sent the next time you open the app.

## Data on Your Device

Your settings, your chosen flower, and any feedback that couldn't be sent are stored on your device. They are removed when you delete the app.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The app does no usage analytics or advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Sharing with Third Parties

Except as required by law, the developer does not share the information it receives with third parties.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the update on this page.
