# SubLog Support

SubLog lets you record the subscriptions you pay for on a page that looks like a grid notebook, and check this month's payments and your next billing dates. It works on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26.5 or later, in English and Japanese.

## Contact

For questions that need a reply, please email support@yutodev.com. If you create the email with Contact Us in the App's settings, the App's version and device details are included.

You can also report bugs or send requests with Send Feedback in the App's settings. Feedback cannot be replied to.

## Frequently asked questions

I bought SubLog Pro, but I can't use it

Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore Purchases in the App.

How to use iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is a SubLog Pro feature. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on every device you want to sync, then turn on iCloud Sync in the App's settings.

Backup and restore

With SubLog Pro, you can create a backup in iCloud Drive and restore from it with Backup & Restore in the App's settings. Restoring adds the backup to your current data without deleting it.

Notifications don't arrive

Make sure notifications for SubLog are allowed in the iOS Settings app and turned on in the App's settings.

I want to delete my data

Reset Data in the App's settings deletes all the subscriptions you have entered. For what is deleted and what remains, see "Deleting data" in the Privacy Policy.
