# SubLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

SubLog ("the App") handles the data it needs to manage your subscriptions on your device and in your own iCloud. The developer does not use any server that collects your activity, nor any advertising, analytics, or crash reporting service.

## 1. Data the App handles

The App handles the subscription details you enter, such as service names, amounts, currencies, billing cycles, next billing dates, categories, notes, URLs, payment methods, free trials, price changes, and how to cancel, together with your notification history and the App's settings.

This data is used only to provide the App's features and is not sent to the developer. The App has no feature that collects your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device identifiers.

## 2. Where data is stored

Your data is stored on your device.

If you turn on iCloud Sync with SubLog Pro, your data is synced through your own private area of Apple's CloudKit.

If you use Backup with SubLog Pro, the backup file is saved to your iCloud Drive (or on your device when iCloud Drive is unavailable).

The developer has no way to see the contents of your iCloud or iCloud Drive. Apple's privacy policy applies to processing by Apple.

## 3. Information the developer receives

The developer receives information from the App only when you send it yourself, in the following two ways.

In-app feedback: the App sends the text you write and its type (bug, request, or other), the App's version and build number, the iOS version, the device model, and your language setting. It does not include your name, contact details, or the subscriptions you have entered. Feedback passes through a Cloudflare server and is stored in the developer's private GitHub repository. It is used only to improve the App and is deleted when no longer needed. To prevent excessive sending, the sender's IP address is used briefly only to count requests and is not stored. Feedback cannot be replied to.

Email inquiries: the content you send to support@yutodev.com from your mail app is used only to reply to you. The draft email the App prepares includes the App's version, the iOS version, and the device model, which you can review and delete before sending.

## 4. Communication with outside services

Exchange rates: to convert between currencies, the App retrieves public exchange rates from FloatRates (www.floatrates.com) at most once a day. This request does not include your data. The service may process your IP address and other information that comes with the connection.

Other than this and the feedback above, the App communicates only with Apple, for iCloud Sync and for purchases and restores through the App Store.

## 5. Features that work only on your device

Add from Screenshots: the App reads service names and amounts from photos you choose, or from screens you capture with the camera, using on-device text recognition (Apple's Vision). Images are neither saved nor sent. Only the photos you choose are read.

Calendar Sync (SubLog Pro): when you turn on Calendar Sync, the App asks for access to your calendars. It finds or creates a calendar named "SubLog", adds billing dates to it as all-day events, and deletes events in that calendar that no longer match your subscriptions. Please do not keep unrelated events in the "SubLog" calendar. Events are not sent to the developer.

App Lock: Face ID, Touch ID, and passcode checks are performed by iOS on your device. Biometric information is never passed to the App or the developer.

Notifications: reminders for billing dates, the end of free trials, subscription anniversaries, and the monthly summary are local notifications on your device.

Widgets, Siri, and Spotlight: widgets show this month's total and upcoming payments, and Siri can tell you this month's total. So that you can find them with Spotlight, the App adds service names, amounts, categories, and payment methods to the search index on your device. The index stays on your device and is removed when you delete the App.

## 6. Purchases

SubLog Pro is purchased and restored through Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive payment information such as card numbers.

## 7. Sharing with third parties and tracking

The developer does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties except as required by law. The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 8. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personally identifiable information from any user, including children.

## 9. Deleting data

You can delete all the subscriptions you have entered with Reset Data in the App's settings. If iCloud Sync is on, they are also deleted from the synced data. Your settings, notification history, cached exchange rates, events added to your calendar, and backup files in iCloud Drive are not deleted by this action.

You can delete calendar events in the Calendar app. You can delete backup files and the App's data stored in iCloud by managing iCloud storage in the iOS Settings app. Deleting the App deletes the App's data on your device.

If you would like feedback you sent to be deleted, please contact support@yutodev.com with the date and content you sent.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When features or the handling of data change, this policy will be updated and the change will be announced on this page.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact support@yutodev.com.
